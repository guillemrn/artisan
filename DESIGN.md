# Design

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Direction

Artisan should feel like a calm operating table for a local business: warm enough for non-technical owners, precise enough for money, stock, orders, pending payments, and daily closing. The visual system should avoid generic SaaS hype, fake social proof, decorative emoji, and overly dramatic motion.

## Landing World

- Palette: warm paper surfaces, deep business green, dark workbench charcoal, muted amber for pending states, and restrained white panels.
- Shape: compact 8px-radius cards and controls, clear separation by bands, no nested cards as page structure.
- Type: existing Manrope and Inter remain the product voice; use weight and scale for hierarchy rather than decorative labels.
- Imagery: the product interface is the primary visual asset. Demonstrate sales, orders, inventory, pending payments, and margin with clearly illustrative demo values.
- Motion: keep interactions quiet and purposeful; no scroll spectacle unless it directly clarifies the product.

## UX Rules

- Spanish copy should stay practical, direct, and owner-friendly.
- Do not invent testimonials, customer names, benchmarks, pricing, or production proof.
- Preserve the current product truth: direct/public customers, point-of-sale customers, stock, cost, public/distributor pricing, tickets, payment state, delivery state, changes, pending payments, consignation, courtesy, and customer orders.
- Future service-business support can be mentioned as direction, but not presented as fully shipped product functionality.
